# Code of Conduct

Angelito Systems is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone who participates in our projects — contributors, maintainers and users alike.

## Our standard

We expect everyone participating in Angelito Systems projects to be respectful, constructive and professional, whether interacting in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while addressing problems directly.
- Protect private information — yours and others'.
- Help create a welcoming space for newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are considered unacceptable within any Angelito Systems project space:

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination in any form.
- Personal attacks, insults or derogatory comments.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or contributions.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit consent.
- Sustained disruptive behavior after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces maintained by Angelito Systems (repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions) and in any other context where an individual is representing an Angelito Systems project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may moderate discussions and contributions to keep communities healthy. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, comments or other content that violates this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict access for behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers using the project's configured contact channel. Reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct reflects Angelito Systems' own standard for participation and is not derived from a third-party template.
